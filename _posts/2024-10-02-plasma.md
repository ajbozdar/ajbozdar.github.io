<span class="dropcap">I</span>t has been about eight years that I [last tried KDE]({{site.url}}/kubuntu/). It was KDE 5 that time, and now we have KDE 6.1. A lot of things have changed. I like [Gnome]({{site.url}}/gnome-light-mode/) because my work flow is fully dependent on Gnome tracker. Thus, I am giving the Plasma Desktop a test run alongside Gnome on Fedora 40.

Plasma offers everything to the user instead of depending on external extensions or whatsoever patches.

## My Favourite Plasma Features
### Plasma Search: Krunner
In 6.1, I found, Plasma krunner got potential to beat Gnome tracker, but it still needs tweaking like all other Plasma features. Yet, it does a much better sorting of files and directories than before.

But there is a problem for me. In both overview and krunner mode, Plasma returns only 10 search results. But, if you try to search through the kickoff menu, it will find results two times more. There must be some kind of tweak in the Plasma settings, and I still need to find it.

### KDE Connect
KDE Connect is something I would love to mention here. It integrates my phone and computer so well that I can nearly forget my phone while at the desk. I can attend calls using on computer, receive notifications, and media transfers are speedier than anything else.

## Exploration Continues
I like to design creativity at KDE. But, I find the Plasma shell a little difficult, because there are tons of options to tweak. Their file manager, dolphin, is missing a few needful features, but they are also adding them to 6.2. The new Plasma overview feature is as killer as in Gnome.

This is all for now about the Plasma desktop. This does not reflect a full KDE experience yet. Let's see if I really switch to KDE. If this happens, KDE will be my third desktop environment on a Unix system.

<span class="dropcap">A</span> new computer center began teaching various technical courses in the neighbourhood recently. Today I visited there for the first time with dad. It was a little far from where we live, but it was all amazing there. All the computers were running Windows98, and the monitors were colourful, unlike at school. Moreover, all the computers were connected to the world wide web.

## The World Wide Web is like a spider's net.
The teacher there said that the world wide web is as same as a spider's net. Everything is connected and information spreads faster than TV. The entire computer web is actually a group of computer networks. On web, we can find a lot of information using a [web browser]({{site.url}}/default-chrome/).

This is the reason dad has so many spider magazines. Well, I already noted the website of my favourite jam before leaving home. I asked my dad if we could learn about the jams on internet. He opened internet explorer which was the default web browser on Windows 98. We saw many kinds of jams on the webpage. We do not have all them in the market. They must be selling them in the big cities.

## What is the Internet?
Teacher said, the internet is all about connecting with others. If we write a mail, it will reach another person's mailbox in minutes. 

We spent more than an hour of time. Dad also paid the internet bill. Internet is not free. It cost us about thirty five rupees; those are about three shillings. It's my three days pocket money. Internet costs too much.

## The Electronic Mail
The electronic mail is called e-mail. It gives you an electronic mailbox. If someone sends you an e-mail from their website, it will come to your mailbox. Yahoo gives e-mail to everybody for free. Dad helped me to create one. Now I can send e-mails to dad, but I cannot write to grandma because they do not have an e-mail address. I will write them a post mail as usual.

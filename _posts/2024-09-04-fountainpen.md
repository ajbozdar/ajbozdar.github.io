<span class="dropcap">A </span> couple of weeks ago, I was considering buying a fountain pen, but I did not find any time to stop by the stationery store. A ball point pen helps to write faster, whereas a fountain pen makes your writing better.

## The Baker's Street
I was out today to see the baker who promised me to bake a cake for me. When I reached his bakery, he told me that he had forgotten. So, I am giving a free sarcastic recommendation that either do not make a baker your friend or do not ask your friend for a cake if he is a baker. You won't get your cake on time.

The only stationery store in my area was not farther than the bakery. I made a choice and turned to the stationery store. There was a collection of fountain pens. He showed me some Dollar Pens. The Dollar pen was always famous in my school. Almost everybody had used it. I also bought a few, [but my dad]({{site.url}}/my-dad-kicked-bombs/) used to buy me a different one.

## The Hero Pen
It was the Hero pen. If I say that the Dollar pen was the finest fountain pen, then the Hero pen was the supreme one. My father had always been worried about my handwriting. I still don't know if it was his choice to buy me the Hero pens or teachers recommended him.

At first, the shopkeeper showed me only Dollar's, but I noticed a different one there too. When I picked it, a known sensation ran through my veins. I looked at it and found that it was a Hero pen. It had not lost the high end quality. I smiled and placed it back. There were a half dozen Dollar's pen waiting for me to choose one. 

I asked the shopkeeper which one is popular among high school students. He said, "it is Bliss fountain pen, Sir." I was not expecting that he would call me, sir. They knew me. I grew up here, but it seems that I look grown up now. I asked him to give me black ink as well.

![dollar-bliss-fountain-pen]({{site.url}}/images/fountain-pen-dollar-bliss.webp)_The Dollar's Bliss Fountain Pen_

## In The End
He packed both the pen and ink for me. I paid the bill, thanked the shopkeeper, and drove back to home. All the way to home, I had nothing in my mind except high school memories. I drove safe, reached home, and now sharing a photo of the Bliss Dollar's fountain pen on the world wide web.

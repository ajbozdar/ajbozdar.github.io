<span class="dropcap">Y</span>esterday it came to my mind to test run Kubutnu. It was not the first time I thought of trying Kubuntu. Many friends have praised KDE for being a decent and full featured desktop. I tried the Plasma desktop on ubuntu first time back in 2012, but I preferred using Unity then.

I am still using Unity, but it feels boring. Unity shell never bothered me, and I witnessed how it evolved through in the last five years.

Well, my overall experience was positive with the  Plasma on Ubuntu. I installed Kubuntu flavour in a VM for maximum KDE experience. KDE search was slower than expected. In Unity, I would simply hit the **Meta** button to begin searching for an app, file, or directory. KDE took time to locate files.

As KDE is a featured pack desktop, it looks like a bloatware to some, but it is not. Unity restricts me from customising the desktop, whereas KDE gives away everything to change whatever I want to do.

KDE also boasts a number of its own software, and that makes it fantastic. I might not switch from Unity to Kubuntu right away, but I have concluded that the Plasma desktop's user experience is much better than Windows.

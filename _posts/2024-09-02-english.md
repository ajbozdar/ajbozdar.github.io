<span class="dropcap">T</span>his is going to be my shortest post I have ever written. In 2017, I took <abbr title="International English Language Testing System">IELTS</abbr> test and got an average band level of 7 out of 9.  This means, my English language skills are at pre-advanced level. According to <abbr title="Common European Framework of Reference for Languages">CEFR</abbr>, it would be C1 level. I wanted to get a band of 8 at least. Recently, some friends reviewed my blogs and told me that there is room for improvement. 😨

## The C2 Level
Now, I have decided to take my English language skills to a new level. For this, I am beginning Macmillan's English book and will finish it before the December, 31st 2024. This is going to be the second personal challenge besides "[100 days to offload]({{site.url}}/100daystooffload/)". I have never taken 2 challenges simultaneously, and that too, when the year end is near around. 😱

![English-test-result-that-was-long-time-ago]({{site.url}}/gifs/english-level-that-was-long-time-ago.gif){:loading="lazy" width="320" height="auto"}_Only 7.0 / That was a long time ago!_

This is going to be a lot of work. The Macmillan English was published in 1982, and for me, it comes from an old bookshop. The book has 10 units, and each unit is about 35 pages long. It focuses on grammar, practical communication, creative expression, and related language skills. I have already skimmed the book, so I am confident that I'll finish it by the end of the year. I have set some time aside in the evening to read and solve the exercises.

## The Last Frontier
Last week, I was considering importing 152 WP blog posts, refine them, and publish here. Now, I would work to improve the language skills and then return to the previous goal. After finishing the book, I will take the C2 exam which is upper advanced level. It is the last level for the masses who use English as a second language, and I'll keep fighting to get there.

This is not going to be easy, but the outcomes will be worthwhile. I wouldn't have started improving my English language skills if I hadn't begun 100 days series. I am grateful to everybody who shared their insights about my writing skills.

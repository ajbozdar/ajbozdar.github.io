<span class="dropcap">F</span>oot fungus is a terrible thing if happens. As much as I could remember, it began in senior high school. My foot began smelling a lot. It won't go away even after soaking both feet in warm water before bed. 🥵

## My First Solution
I turned to a friend who was a practicing doctor. He advised, if I mix a teaspoon of salt in warm water and soak both feet. It would go away as salt reduces the pore size in the skin to cut the sweating down. According to him, it's a minor invisible fungus. It develops because of excessive sweating and the various materials used to make shoes. The solution worked for me, and I still continue it.

But avoid prolonging the use of salt. It would seize the skin pores altogether. This may become dangerous than fungus itself. 

## Second Solution
This is my finding. I use two insoles in my shoes now. Factory made shoes have single thin insole which can't suck away the sweat flooding out of my both feet. Thus, it creates fungus and smells awful. Six months ago, it came to my mind why not to try another insole over factory made insole. You might find a variety of insoles in the market, but all work similarly. 

Now the upper insoles suck up all the sweating, and this way fungus doesn't go deep down inside the shoe.

Least sweating, no fungus, no smell. 😰

My all shoe pairs have two insoles now. Whenever I buy a new pair, I also buy an insole. I also do not wear socks while my foot are wet. I also keep the toe spaces dry as much as possible. 

And that's all. ☺️

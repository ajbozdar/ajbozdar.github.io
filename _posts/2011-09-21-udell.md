<span class="dropcap">W</span>hat you see in the above image is Ubuntu 10.04 LTS running on Dell. It came pre-installed on Dell Vostro 1450. I never expected that Ubuntu would come pre-installed on a laptop, but here we are. The times are changing now.

I can notice that there are more apps in Ubuntu repositories than before. For now, I am not sure if my lab's printer will work with Ubuntu or not.

I like Gnome's gbrainy game. It is fantastic to play. GCC and GDB are also working fine. I used Mozilla's Thunderbird to sync my emails. Pidgin is awesome in Ubuntu.

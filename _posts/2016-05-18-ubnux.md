<span class="dropcap">I</span> did a clean installation of Ubuntu 2016 LTS. The system UI looks much better than before.

In the past four years, Ubuntu has evolved a lot. Many people have criticised the Unity Desktop environment, but they can always use a different one. Many people disliked Amazon services in recent years.

Unity, once looked cluttered due to pesky Amazon adverts, is much better now. I do not have any benefits from Amazon as it doesn't operate in my country, so I removed it since the day one. 

As always, I have to remove all games except the gbrainy.

I will keep using Ubuntu as it is, but I want to give KDE one more try. I am going to test KDE in a <abbr title="Virtual Machine">VM</abbr>. KDE uses Plasma desktop. I have heard many people praising the quality of KDE features.

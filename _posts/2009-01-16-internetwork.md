All connected devices are called **hosts** or **end systems**.

Hosts or end systems are connected by a network of **communication links** and 
**packet switches**.

Transmission rate is measured in **bits per second**.

Data **packets** are sent over the network like letters inside envelops. 

Two kind of packet switches are used **routers** and **link-layer switches**.

The sequence of communication links are called **route** or **path**.

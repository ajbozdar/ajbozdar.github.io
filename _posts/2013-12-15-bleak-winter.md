<span class="dropcap">T</span>he poet's full name is Christina Georgina Rossetti. She was a famous, late 19th century poet. She used to write for kids. She was a religious lady and wrote many songs for Christians. 

Her following poem is my one of the favourite among many. 

## In The Bleak Mid-winter

In the bleak mid-winter  
Frosty wind made moan,  
Earth stood hard as iron,  
Water like a stone;  
Snow had fallen, snow on snow,  
Snow on snow,  
In the bleak mid-winter  
Long ago.  

A Poem By C.G. Rossetti

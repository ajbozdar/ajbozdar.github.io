<span class="dropcap">I</span> am an Ubuntu <abbr title="Long Term Support">LTS</abbr> user. In 12.04 LTS, Ubuntu has introduced a new desktop environment. It looks stable and better than what I heard from word of mouth.

I like the way it helps me to find files and folders. There is hype over Ubuntu forums and everywhere on the internet. 

I am not a  fan of Ubuntu games on this new dashboard, so I have removed them in one command.

{% highlight shell %}
$ sudo apt-get purge gnome-games-common && sudo apt-get autoremove
{% endhighlight %}

Everything else looks better, including the office suite. It's the amazing Libre Office. A free of cost office suite!
